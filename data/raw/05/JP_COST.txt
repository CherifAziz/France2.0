Costs and cost-effectiveness of each ministry's information system

    

  

        Last Updated:

      Jun 26, 2026

  

            

Overview
Based on the "Priority Plan for the Realization of a Digital Society" (decided by the Cabinet on June 21, 2024), we will promote efforts to "visualize" information systems expenses and cost effectiveness in order for people to feel the improvement of convenience through digitalization and its results. 
Expenditures for Information Systems at the Cabinet Office and Each Ministry (Settlement Amount)
It publishes the actual information system expenses of each office and ministry.

Expenses for Information Systems at the Cabinet Office and Each Ministry (Settlement Amount) (FY 2024)
Progress toward the 30% reduction target
The progress of the 30% reduction target for government information system operating expenses, etc., based on the settlement of accounts, is published.
